Privacy

Last updated August 24, 2026

This is my personal portfolio site. I keep data collection light, but a few services process visitor data on my behalf, and I want to be upfront about what they see.

Analytics (PostHog)

I use PostHog (US Cloud) to understand how the site is used. It records page views, clicks on things like project cards and social links, your browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. PostHog keeps its identifier in memory only, so nothing is stored on your device between visits, and it may capture a session replay: an anonymized recording of how the page is scrolled and clicked during your visit. I do not use analytics to identify you personally. If your browser sends the Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, analytics is not loaded at all.

Contact form (FormSubmit)

If you enter your email in the contact form, it is delivered to my inbox by FormSubmit. I use it only to reply to you.

Booking (Cal.com)

The calendar page embeds Cal.com. Anything you enter while booking (name, email, notes) is handled by Cal.com under its own privacy policy and lands in my calendar.

Hosting (Vercel)

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs and provides cookieless, aggregate visit counts.

Fonts (Google Fonts)

Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser requests font files from Google servers, which see your IP address as part of serving the request.

Questions or removal

Email me at aprathapa01@gmail.com if you want to know what any of these services recorded about your visit or want something deleted, and I will follow up with the relevant service.
